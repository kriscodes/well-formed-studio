04 / Good to know

A few details,
well defined.

What counts as one defect?

A specific, reproducible problem with one agreed outcome, estimated to take 60–90 minutes including implementation and verification. A request with several unrelated issues or substantial unknowns may fall outside this offer.

Can an agency request a repair for a client?

Yes, provided the agency has authority to request the change and grant the necessary access. That authority, the scope, and who can approve review and publication must be confirmed before payment.

Does 60–90 minutes mean same-day delivery?

No. It is an estimate of the work involved, not a turnaround commitment. Capacity, a delivery timeframe, required access, and the review and publication process are agreed before payment.

What if the problem is bigger than expected?

Work stays within the agreed scope. If new information changes feasibility or scope, the next step needs a separate agreement. This offer does not include an open-ended redesign or ongoing support.

How will payment work?

After a repair is reviewed and agreed, a private link will take you to Stripe’s hosted checkout for $195 USD. Payment is currently unavailable while the studio prepares to open. There is no public pay-before-review checkout.

Can I send a request now?

Not yet. The studio is in prelaunch, and a business contact channel is still being prepared. Enquiries and payments are closed; this page does not collect submissions or payment details.
